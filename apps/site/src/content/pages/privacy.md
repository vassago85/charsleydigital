## Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you contact us (name, organisation, email, phone, and project details). We use this solely to respond to enquiries and provide services.

## How we use it

## Data storage

Data is stored securely. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact us at hello@charsleydigital.co.za.
