About Us

Simplifying Compliance for the Public

Ranyati Firearm Motivations has been in operation since 2006, having facilitated tens of thousands of successful applications. We offer a comprehensive service tailored to the administration of the Firearms Control Act, simplifying compliance for the public.

Each motivation addresses all the reasons historically used by the Central Firearm Registrar (CFR) to reject applications, accompanied by the necessary supporting documentation required by your local SAPS. Our meticulous office review ensures thoroughness.

Once completed, the motivation and supporting documents are compiled into a professionally bound book — ready for submission.

The Ranyati Motivation Book

  • Professionally prepared motivation letter
  • Completed licence application forms
  • All required supporting documentation
  • Addresses all known CFR rejection reasons
  • Compiled into a professionally bound book

Our Process

Four simple steps to your licence

We've streamlined the application process so you can focus on what matters.

1

Consultation

Contact us and discuss your specific needs. We'll assess your situation and advise on the best approach for your firearm licence application.

2

Preparation

We prepare your motivation letter and complete all application forms on your behalf. Every known reason for CFR rejection is proactively addressed in your documentation.

3

Review & Compile

Our office conducts a meticulous review of all documentation. Once approved, everything is compiled into a professionally bound book ready for submission.

4

Submit

Take your professionally prepared application book to your local SAPS for submission. Your application is thorough, complete, and ready to go.
