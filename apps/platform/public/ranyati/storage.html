About Us

Registered Firearm Dealer Since Establishment

Ranyati Firearms Safe Storage is a registered Firearm Dealer with Licence number 100939, specialising in the trade of second-hand firearms, including firearm exports. Situated in Garsfontein, Pretoria, we provide comprehensive services for the safe custody, transportation, and transfer of ownership of firearms and ammunition, particularly dealer stock.

We excel in the comprehensive management of firearms within deceased estates, offering a fully integrated turnkey solution tailored to meet the unique needs of each estate.

Our Services

Comprehensive firearm management

From safe custody to estate administration, we handle every aspect of firearm management with professionalism and care.

Safe Custody

Secure storage of firearms and ammunition in our purpose-built, licensed facility. Full compliance with all regulatory requirements.

Transportation

Licensed and secure transportation of firearms and ammunition. Professional handling throughout the entire process.

Transfer of Ownership

Full administration of firearm ownership transfers, ensuring all legal requirements are met and properly documented.

Second-hand Firearm Trade

As a registered dealer, we facilitate the buying and selling of second-hand firearms through proper legal channels.

Firearm Exports

Expert handling of firearm export processes, including all permits, documentation, and regulatory compliance for international transfers.

Dealer Stock Management

Secure storage and management of dealer stock, including comprehensive inventory tracking and regulatory compliance.

Deceased Estates

Comprehensive Estate Firearm Management

We excel in the comprehensive management of firearms within deceased estates, offering a fully integrated turnkey solution tailored to meet the unique needs of each estate.

In cases where heirs are not interested in inheriting firearms, we are able to offer a solution. Executors handling the estate of deceased registered firearm owners can instruct Ranyati Firearm Safe Storage to arrange firearm collection at the onset of the administration process.

Speak to an Expert

Collection at Onset

We arrange firearm collection from the estate at the very beginning of the administration process.

Secure Storage During Administration

All firearms are securely stored in our licensed facility while the estate is being administered.

Transfer or Disposal

We manage the transfer of firearms to heirs or, where heirs are not interested, facilitate sale or disposal through legal channels.

Turnkey Solution

A fully integrated end-to-end service, from initial collection to final resolution of all estate firearms.
